# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in FoxMakers open-source projects a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

✅ **Acceptable:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the maker community

❌ **Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others private information
- Other conduct which could be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report violations to [info@foxmakers.it](mailto:info@foxmakers.it).  
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
